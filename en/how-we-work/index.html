How we work.

Learning through dialogue, not monologue.

From an internal workshop with our team.

Process to be confirmed

From enquiry to badge.

  1. 1

    You get in touch

    You describe your team, your goal, roughly how many people will take part and when would suit you.

    Enquiry form

  2. 2

    We agree the details

    Together we choose the module, format and date. Modules can be combined or tailored to you.

    online or in person

  3. 3

    Your team learns

    During the training you work on examples from your own working day, with time for questions and trying things out.

    from 4 hours · max. 16 participants

  4. 4

    You show what you can do

    Individual stages of the learning path earn digital badges as proof of learning.

    Open Badges

Principles to be confirmed

What to expect from our training.

  1. 01

    Easy to get started

    No technical background needed. We start wherever your team is today.

  2. 02

    Small groups

    No more than 16 participants, online or in person – so there is time for questions and hands-on practice.

  3. 03

    Proof of learning

    Our learning path awards digital badges, for example for AI literacy under the EU AI Act and for prompt engineering.

Proof of learning to be confirmed

Skills you can show.

Our AI training series is designed as a 64-hour learning path. Individual stages earn digital badges, such as “KI-Kompetenzen nach dem EU AI Act” (AI literacy under the EU AI Act) and “Prompt Engineering”.
