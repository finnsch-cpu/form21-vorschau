About us.

As an education company based in Berlin, we combine many years of expertise with technological know-how.

01Who we are

Developing skills and finding them.

We help companies recruit young talent on the basis of skills and get their existing teams ready for the shift to AI. Developing skills and matching them are how we make that happen.

Form21 was founded by Franziska Schmid and Dr Julia Kleeberger, who also founded Junge Tüftler*innen and mycelia. We offer AI training for teams, the STARK career orientation app, and consulting for organisations going through transformation and people development.

mycelia gGmbH: An independent non-profit organisation with the same managing directors. mycelia makes skills visible, for example with Open Educational Badges, and connects education with climate action. to be confirmed
