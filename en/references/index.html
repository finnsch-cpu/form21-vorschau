Pilot project · career orientation in Brandenburg

Talentpfad Lausitz

Together with AWO Bezirksverband Brandenburg Süd e.V., Form21 gives young people in Lusatia structured access to hands-on experience in care and social professions, supported digitally through the STARK app.

Delivered under the guideline “Förderung sozialer Innovationen im Land Brandenburg 2023” of the Brandenburg Ministry of Economic Affairs, Labour and Energy. Funded by the European Union and the State of Brandenburg.
