More about our approach

Context

AI literacy and the EU AI Act.

Article 4 of the EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, addresses the AI literacy of staff who work with AI systems.

What this means in practice for your organisation is the focus of our module AI Literacy & the EU AI Act. The training is not a substitute for legal advice.
