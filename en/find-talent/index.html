Finding skills · Career orientation for training companies

Find talent with STARK.

STARK is an AI-powered career orientation web app and a project of Form21 GmbH. School students and career changers discover their strengths there. Your company becomes visible where they look for training places.

  • For employers

    See the employer demo

    Three steps: post a position, appear in the app, receive a message.

    Enquire about visibility

  • For employers · your own access to be confirmed

    STARK partner portal (external website)

    Features, packages and registration. We cannot promise in advance whether, or how many, young people will get in touch.

  • For students and schools

    Go to the STARK app (external website)

    Are you a student, a teacher or a school? You will find the STARK app and everything you need to use it in class on the STARK website, which is in German.

Where STARK works with partners

Two examples where the contribution of STARK is publicly described.

Talentpfad Lausitz

A pilot project by Form21 and AWO Bezirksverband Brandenburg Süd e.V. It starts in September 2026 and aims to make it easier for young people in Lusatia to enter care and social professions. There are no results yet.

View the project

KISEE

A research project by the non-profit mycelia gGmbH and the Institute for Applied AI at Stuttgart Media University. According to mycelia, the ESCO-based interest analysis from STARK feeds into the evaluation. mycelia is an independent organisation with the same managing directors.
